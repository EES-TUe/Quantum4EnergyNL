Symposium: The role of quantum technologies in the energy transition

The Quantum4EnergyNL initiative is organizing a full-day symposium on the role of quantum technologies (computing, sensing, communications) in the energy transition. Researchers and professionals from academia, industry, and policy interested in the interface of energy systems and quantum technologies are warmly invited to learn about relevant work in the Netherlands and to meet the community.

The workshop brings together experts approaching power and energy systems from both the ICT and electrical-engineering domains, alongside quantum-technology and algorithm researchers interested in power and energy applications of their work.

Attendance costs €25, which covers lunch and networking drinks and helps us keep no-shows down. There is room for up to 90 participants; places are allocated on a first-come basis. Registration closes on 28 October 2026, or earlier if the symposium is full.
